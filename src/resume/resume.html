Keywords it uses that already match something true in your profile.json (skills, experience, projects) get surfaced into the resume as an extra ATS-scannable line — nothing is fabricated. Keywords with no match in your profile are listed as gaps for you to review, never inserted automatically. Your resume PDF is the source: its text and structure are read back into LaTeX, previewed here as rendered A4 pages (old vs. new), and written back out as a PDF via "Save as PDF". The PDF this extension already has is picked up automatically — upload another below to replace it. Nothing is uploaded anywhere by this page.
