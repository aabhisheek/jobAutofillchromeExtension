Profile

The single source of truth used to fill applications. Sensitive fields (sponsorship, work authorization, EEO) live under answers — leave any of them blank to force the extension to flag that question as unmatched instead of guessing.

src/data/profile.json ships with the extension and is the fallback whenever nothing is saved yet (fresh install, cleared storage), so you don't re-type your profile after unrelated code changes. Save to update your live copy; Download to export it back over the file in source control.

Learned field rules

When the AI answers a field your dictionary doesn't recognise, it can report which part of your profile it used. That mapping is filed here as a rule, so the same question is answered from your profile next time without an AI call. Rules are mappings, never answers — correcting your profile corrects every rule pointing at it. Nothing applies until you approve it.

Elsewhere

AI Providers Keys and models for drafting answers — Groq, Gemini, DeepSeek, OpenRouter, Together, Mistral, xAI, Claude, OpenAI, or your own local model. Applications Every job you saved or applied to, with the pipeline board and response rate.

Analytics

Counts and ATS platform names only — never a field value, your profile, a job description, a resume, an API key, or the URL of a page you visit. Events carry a random per-install id generated locally, and IP geolocation is disabled.
